# Delete your data — Bean Slinger

Bean Slinger has no accounts you sign in to. The only information we hold about you online
is what you chose to post to the **Global Leaderboard**. This page explains how to delete it.

## What can be deleted

If you posted Overtime scores to the Global Leaderboard, we hold:

- your **nickname**;
- your **scores**, with the run details used to check them (line, run length, beans sorted,
  gold banked, and when they were posted);
- any **reports** you made about other players' nicknames;
- the **anonymous ID** the Game created for you. It is not linked to your email, phone or
  Google account.

Deleting removes **all of it permanently**. Nothing is kept afterwards, and it cannot be
undone.

## Option 1: in the Game (instant)

1. Open Bean Slinger.
2. On the main menu, tap the round **i** button.
3. Tap **LEADERBOARD**.
4. Tap **DELETE MY LEADERBOARD DATA**, then tap it again to confirm.

Everything is deleted straight away, including the anonymous ID.

## Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the Game)

Email **danjonlang91@hotmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my Bean Slinger data"**. Include:

- the **nickname** you used on the leaderboard;
- roughly **when** you last posted a score, and on which line (3, 4 or 5 lanes), if you
  remember.

Because we do not know your name or email, we find your data by that nickname. We will
delete it and confirm by reply within **one month**, normally much sooner.

## Other data

- **Game progress** (levels, gold, trophies) is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the
  Game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it. We never receive a copy.
- **Advertising data** is collected and held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete
  your Advertising ID in your phone's settings (*Settings → Privacy → Ads*; the path varies
  by device). You can manage the rest through Google's own controls:
  https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

For everything else, see the full [Privacy Policy](../).
